Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 11:41–44, One More Morning celebrates the sure promise of the resurrection. Every soul will rise, body and spirit reunited, and every life will continue beyond the grave. With an uplifting, groove-driven pop sound, the song reminds us that because death is not the end, our choices, our love, and our faith carry eternal significance. Hope is certain, mercy is real, and every morning points to the greatest one still to come.
