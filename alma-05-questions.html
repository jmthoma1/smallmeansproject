Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 5, Questions captures the Savior's invitation to examine the heart through Alma's searching questions. Rather than offering easy answers, the song updates those timeless questions for today, inviting listeners to pause, reflect, and honestly consider who they are becoming. Set to an intimate, piano-driven alternative rock sound, it reminds us that the right questions often become the first steps toward faith, change, and coming closer to Jesus Christ.
