Behind the song

The Inspiration

Enos carries the weight of his mistakes into a quiet forest, seeking answers he cannot find anywhere else. Through a long night of prayer, faith, and personal struggle, he discovers that God’s mercy is greater than his fears. Forgiven and changed, his thoughts turn to others, and he pleads for future generations to know the same Savior. A timeless story of repentance, redemption, and the peace that comes from hearing the Lord’s voice.
