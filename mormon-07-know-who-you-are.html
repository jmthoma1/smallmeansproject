Behind the song

The Inspiration

A vibrant Latin-pop anthem inspired by Mormon's message to future generations: remember your heritage, lay down your weapons and come unto Christ. Warm Latino male vocals with a natural Spanish accent, rhythmic reggaeton percussion, Spanish guitar and bilingual lyrics create a joyful celebration of faith and identity. Though his civilization has fallen, Mormon's message lives on, reminding future generations of who they are and the Savior who awaits them.
