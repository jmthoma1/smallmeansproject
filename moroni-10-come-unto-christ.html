Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s final invitation to remember God’s mercy, seek truth through the Holy Ghost, recognize the gifts God gives His children, and ultimately come unto Christ and be perfected in Him. The song gathers the great themes of the Book of Mormon into one final call: remember, ask, believe, receive, and come to the Savior. It is meant to feel like an ending that opens into something new—the record closes, but the invitation continues.
