Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 18, “Before I Say a Word” reflects how Ammon’s character reached King Lamoni before his teaching ever began. Through faithful service, courage, humility, and genuine love, Ammon lived what he believed—and Lamoni noticed. With joyful, female-led Memphis soul and energetic call-and-response, the song reminds us to let our lives speak first: live our faith, love others well, and when they ask us why, then say the word
