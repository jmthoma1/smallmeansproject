Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 35, “What Makes You Mad?” pokes fun at the absurd anger of the Zoramites: they cast out those who believed, only to become furious when the people of Ammon welcomed, fed, clothed, and cared for them. Set to quirky, high-energy new-wave dance rock, the song asks a simple question: if you didn’t want them, why are you angry that somebody else does? Sometimes another person’s compassion reveals just how unreasonable our resentment has become.
