Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mosiah 18:8–10, “Together” celebrates the covenant Alma invited his people to make at the Waters of Mormon. More than a song about baptism, it is a promise to bear one another’s burdens, mourn with those who mourn, comfort those in need, and walk together as disciples of Jesus Christ. In simple harmony, it reminds us that faith is not meant to be lived alone, but in a community bound together by love and covenant.
