Behind the song

The Inspiration

Jacob 5 tells the story of the Lord of the vineyard laboring patiently to save and preserve every branch of His vineyard. Through seasons of pruning, nourishing, grafting, and gathering, He never gives up on His work or His people. In the end, the scattered branches are brought together, the fruit is preserved, and they become like one body. This song celebrates unity, gathering, perseverance, and the joy of laboring with the Lord until all things are brought together in Him.
