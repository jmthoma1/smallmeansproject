The Inspiration

Jacob 7 tells the story of a prophet whose faith could not be shaken. Faced with opposition, arguments, and demands for signs, Jacob stood firmly on the witness he had already received from God. His confidence did not come from winning debates, but from sacred experiences with the Savior that had settled deeply in his soul.
