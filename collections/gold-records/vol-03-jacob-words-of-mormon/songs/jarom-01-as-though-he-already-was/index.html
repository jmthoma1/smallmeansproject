The Inspiration

Jarom lived in a time when generations waited for the promised Messiah, yet the faithful did not treat Christ as a distant hope. They believed in Him, worshipped Him, and followed His teachings “as though he already was.” Their faith reached beyond the limits of time because they trusted the eternal Son of God—the Great I AM.
