Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the faithful women mentioned in Nephi 5:6 who chose to follow Nephi because they “believed in the warnings and revelations of God.” “A Place for Us” imagines the courage, sacrifice, and hope of leaving behind conflict to build homes, families, faith, and a future in the promised land. Through harmony and trust in the Lord, they find belonging, peace, and a covenant place where generations can grow in light.
