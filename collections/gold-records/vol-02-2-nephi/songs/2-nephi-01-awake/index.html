Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Lehi’s final plea to his children in Second Nephi Chapter 1 to “awake,” “arise from the dust,” and “shake off the chains” that bind them. This song captures the urgency of choosing liberty, light, and covenant faithfulness over spiritual captivity. A high-energy anthem about waking up spiritually, standing for truth, and refusing to let darkness win.
