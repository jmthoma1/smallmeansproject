Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 26, No One Is Left Out celebrates Christ’s open invitation to every soul. Nephi asks a series of powerful questions: Does the Lord turn anyone away? Does He forbid anyone from His goodness? The answer is always the same—Nay. With themes of grace, belonging, and redemption, the song proclaims that Christ invites all to come unto Him, regardless of background, status, race, or circumstance. No barriers. No exclusions. No one is left out.
