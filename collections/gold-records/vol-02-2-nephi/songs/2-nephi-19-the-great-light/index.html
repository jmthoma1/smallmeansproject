Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 19 (Isaiah 9), The Great Light is a cowboy campfire spiritual about finding hope after a long season of darkness. Drawing on Isaiah’s imagery of burdens lifted, dawn breaking, and a promised Deliverer, the song follows weary travelers who discover that the light they have been searching for is not merely a new day, but the coming of the Savior. With gratitude, harmony, and quiet joy, they gather around the fire to testify: “Unto us a Son.”
