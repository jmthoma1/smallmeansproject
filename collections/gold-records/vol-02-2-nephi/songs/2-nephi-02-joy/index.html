Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Lehi’s teachings to Jacob in 2 Nephi 2:25 that “men are, that they might have joy.” This song reflects the love of a Father who continues reaching for His children through sorrow, agency, opposition, and redemption. A hopeful anthem about divine love, belonging, and the joy made possible through the Savior, even in life’s hardest moments.
