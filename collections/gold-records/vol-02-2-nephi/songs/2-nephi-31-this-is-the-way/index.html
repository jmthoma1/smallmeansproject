Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 31, This Is the Way is a joyful Celtic folk anthem about choosing to follow Jesus Christ. Drawing on Nephi’s teachings about baptism, discipleship, the Holy Ghost, and enduring to the end, the song imagines life as a journey along a narrow road marked by the Savior’s footprints. Through storms, valleys, and uncertainty, His example lights the path forward. With soaring fiddle, whistle, and communal harmonies, it celebrates the simple yet profound truth: Christ has shown the way.
