Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 14 (Isaiah 4), “Cloud and Fire” looks forward to the Millennial reign of Christ, when the Branch of the Lord will be beautiful and glorious, the earth will be renewed, and the faithful remnant will be called holy. Just as ancient Israel was guided through the wilderness by a cloud and a pillar of fire, all nations will be led by the Lord’s light. Zion will become a place of refuge, peace, and divine protection beneath His everlasting covering.
