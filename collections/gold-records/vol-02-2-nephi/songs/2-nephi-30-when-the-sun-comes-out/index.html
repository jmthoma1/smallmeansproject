Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 30, When the Sun Comes Out celebrates the joy of seeing clearly after a long night of confusion and darkness. Drawing on Nephi’s imagery of scales falling from the eyes and all hidden things being revealed, the song compares spiritual awakening to sunrise. What once seemed lost, tangled, or concealed becomes visible in the light. With bright vintage harmonies and an uplifting spirit, it rejoices in the truth that darkness cannot hide forever when the light of Christ appears.
