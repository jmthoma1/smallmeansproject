Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 13 (Isaiah 3), this song presents Isaiah as a dramatic storyteller warning a society that has traded wisdom for pride and truth for applause. Through vivid imagery and theatrical energy, he shows how vanity, selfishness, and the pursuit of status lead to confusion and collapse. Yet beneath the warning is a message of hope: those who choose righteousness need not fear, for the Lord remembers them, and it shall be well with them.
