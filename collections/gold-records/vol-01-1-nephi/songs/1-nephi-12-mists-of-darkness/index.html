Behind the song

The Inspiration

“Mists of Darkness” is inspired by 1 Nephi 12:4; 16-18 and the vision of those who lose their way in the fog of pride, temptation, and spiritual blindness. Written as a dark cinematic folk ballad, the song follows souls reaching for light while others disappear into the mist. It’s a warning, a lament, and a reminder that the rod, the tree, and the mercy of God are still there for those willing to press forward.
