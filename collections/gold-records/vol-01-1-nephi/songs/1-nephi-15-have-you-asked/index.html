Behind the song

The Inspiration

“Have You Asked?” is inspired by 1 Nephi 15:8 and Nephi’s powerful question to his brothers: “Have ye inquired of the Lord?” Blending soul-rock energy with fiery female vocals, the song is a call to seek revelation instead of surrendering to fear and doubt. Through driving rhythms, gospel intensity, and raw conviction, it reminds us that heaven still speaks to those willing to ask, believe, and listen.
