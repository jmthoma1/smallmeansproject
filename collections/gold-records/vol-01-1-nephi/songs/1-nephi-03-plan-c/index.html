Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 1 Nephi 3, “Plan C” looks beyond the familiar promise to “go and do” and asks what happens when doing doesn’t go according to plan. Fast, playful ska turns setbacks into momentum: Plan A fails, Plan B goes worse, but faith keeps moving. The destination can stay the same even when the route changes. You don’t need to know every step before you begin—sometimes commitment means laughing at the wreckage, getting back up, and trying Plan C.
