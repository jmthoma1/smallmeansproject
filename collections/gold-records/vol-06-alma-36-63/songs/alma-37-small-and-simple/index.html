Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 37:6–7, “Small and Simple” celebrates the truth at the heart of the Small Means Project: great things often begin almost unnoticed. A word, a seed, a prayer, a song, a willing heart—God can use small means to accomplish things far greater than their beginnings suggest. With joyful zydeco-pop energy, the song reminds us we don’t need thunder or spectacle. Faith grows, lives change, and great things come to pass a little at a time.
