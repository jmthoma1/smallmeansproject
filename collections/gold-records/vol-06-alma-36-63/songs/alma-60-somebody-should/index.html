Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 60, “Somebody Should” channels Moroni’s frustration with people who have the responsibility and ability to help but fail to act. Set to a dramatic tango pop-rock groove, the song turns that challenge toward modern life: it’s easy to notice problems, assign blame, and wait for someone else to step in. We can’t solve everything, but when a need is within our reach, responsibility becomes personal. Sometimes the “somebody” we’re waiting for is us.
