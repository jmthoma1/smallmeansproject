Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 58, “Enough for Today” reflects the experience of Helaman and his army when desperately needed reinforcements failed to arrive. Their circumstances remained difficult, but prayer brought assurance, peace, faith, and renewed strength to move forward. Through intimate piano-driven alternative pop, the song explores a deeply practical kind of faith: we may not receive everything needed for the road ahead all at once, but God can give us the strength, hope, and courage we need for today.
