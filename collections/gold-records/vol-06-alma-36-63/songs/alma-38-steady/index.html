Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 38 and Shiblon’s quiet faithfulness, “Steady” celebrates the power of staying true to a course. A ship reaches its destination by holding its heading; a mountain is climbed one step at a time; a life is shaped choice by choice. Propulsive electronica and melodic dubstep turn that consistency into a pulse that keeps moving forward. Faith doesn’t always require a dramatic leap—sometimes it means trusting God, doing the work, and staying steady.
