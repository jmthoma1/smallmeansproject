Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 36 and Alma’s conversion, “Have Mercy on Me” follows his journey from overwhelming guilt to overwhelming joy. Remembering his father’s teachings of Christ, Alma cries out to Jesus with the little hope he has—and everything changes. The gospel-soul arrangement mirrors that transformation, beginning in anguish and bursting into celebration as “Have mercy on me” becomes “He had mercy on me.” A testimony that no darkness is beyond the reach of Christ’s mercy
