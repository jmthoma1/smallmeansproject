Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 55, “Your Own Two Hands” draws from Moroni’s decision to give imprisoned Nephites the tools they needed to take part in their own deliverance. Driven by energetic progressive bluegrass, the song explores a broader lesson about helping others: offer your knowledge, time, encouragement, and every useful tool you can—but don’t become the only one doing the work. The best help doesn’t create dependence; it helps someone discover what they can accomplish with their own two hands.
