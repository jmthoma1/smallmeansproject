Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 46, **“Raise the Standard”** is told through the eyes of Captain Moroni as he watches his nation divide and chooses to act. Fusing cinematic war music with modern rap, the song transforms the Title of Liberty into a rallying cry to stand for faith, freedom, family, and truth. Moroni's torn coat becomes more than a banner—it becomes a challenge to every generation to decide what is worth defending before the battle ever begins.
