Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 43:45, “A Better Cause” explores the difference between fighting with anger and standing for something worth protecting. Home, family, freedom, and faith give courage a purpose that fury alone cannot sustain. Driven by fast Irish punk and Celtic rock, the male/female duet turns that shared conviction into an anthem: when the struggle gets hard, knowing what you stand for can keep you standing. **Not just a cause—a better cause.**
