Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 57, **“Little Things”** draws from Helaman’s description of his young warriors obeying every word of command with exactness. Blending modern male/female hip-hop with Tuvan-inspired rhythms and vocals, the song celebrates the quiet discipline behind extraordinary results. Great outcomes rarely begin with one heroic moment—they grow from small choices repeated faithfully: keeping your word, showing up, following through, and doing things right even when nobody is watching.
