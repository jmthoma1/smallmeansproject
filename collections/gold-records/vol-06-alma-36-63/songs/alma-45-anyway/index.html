Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 45:10–14, “Anyway” explores the strange human habit of knowing exactly where a choice leads and making it anyway. From touching a hot stove to holding onto grudges and habits that hurt us, knowledge alone doesn’t guarantee wisdom. Driven by twitchy, modern art-funk and new-wave energy, the song finds humor in our everyday self-sabotage before landing on a harder truth: sometimes we see the warning, understand the cost, know better—and choose it anyway.
