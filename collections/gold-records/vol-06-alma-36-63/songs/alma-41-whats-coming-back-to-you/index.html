Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 41, “Can’t Call It Sweet” takes Alma’s teaching on restoration and gives it some swagger: changing the label doesn’t change the truth, and what we send into the world has a way of coming back. Big-band swing collides with rapid-fire rap as bitter stays bitter, mercy returns mercy, and good comes back good. The question underneath all the brass and attitude is simple: **what are you sending out—and what’s coming back to you?**
