Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 56, “God Is With Us” captures the childlike faith of Helaman’s two thousand young warriors as they turn fearlessly toward battle. Far from their mothers, they carry the lessons learned at their knees: God hears them, God knows them, and they do not walk alone. Driven by thunderous taiko drums, the song celebrates the moment a mother’s faith becomes her child’s own—when what was taught at home becomes courage strong enough to carry into the unknown.
