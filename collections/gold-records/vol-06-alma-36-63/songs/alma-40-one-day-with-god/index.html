Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 40:8, “One Day with God” challenges our need to measure everything by minutes, years, deadlines, and calendars. Alma reminds us that time is measured by humanity, while eternity belongs to a perspective far beyond our clocks. Driven by fast melodic punk energy, the song trades anxiety about *when* for confidence in what God has promised. We count the seconds; God sees beyond them. Eternity doesn’t wear a watch.
