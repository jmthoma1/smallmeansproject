Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 42, “I Choose” explores the balance between agency, justice, and mercy. We are free to choose our path, but our choices carry real consequences—without them, choice would have little meaning. Justice ensures our decisions matter, while mercy leaves room to turn and choose again. Driven by gritty 1960s British R&B with an urgent female vocal, the song celebrates both responsibility and redemption: I chose then. I choose now. And I choose again.
