Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 62, “No Time for This” captures the weary determination of people forced to confront problems they thought they had already solved. With a fast talking-blues groove and dry humor, the song follows the drive to restore order, get back to the real work, and stop letting recurring trouble consume precious time. But Teancum’s final act adds a warning: decisive action and reckless impatience can look remarkably similar. Sometimes strength means acting now; wisdom means knowing when.
