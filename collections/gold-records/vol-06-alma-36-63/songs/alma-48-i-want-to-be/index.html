Behind the song

The Inspiration

**Inspired by Alma 48, "I Want to Be" reflects on the kind of person worth becoming. Rather than celebrating victories or fame, the song longs for quiet strength, unwavering integrity, faithful preparation, and the courage to protect, serve, and stand for what is right. Driven by powerful modern pop-rock, it transforms Captain Moroni's timeless qualities into a personal anthem, reminding us that character is built one choice, one day, and one life at a time.**
