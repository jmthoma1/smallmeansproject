Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 44, “When You Could” explores the strength it takes to show restraint when you hold the advantage. Moroni has the power to destroy his enemy, but instead offers a way out. Driven by tight, swaggering 1980s funk-rock, the song asks what we do when nothing prevents us from settling the score. Real power isn’t proven by using every advantage we have—sometimes it’s proven by what we choose not to do when we could.
