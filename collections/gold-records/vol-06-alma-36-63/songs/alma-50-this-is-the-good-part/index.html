Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 50:22–23, “This Is the Good Part” celebrates finding happiness in the middle of an imperfect life. Despite wars and difficulties, Mormon describes this as an extraordinarily happy time among the Nephites. With bright, female-led indie pop and Afro-pop energy, the song brings that idea into everyday life: burnt toast, busy schedules, noisy homes, and people we love. Don’t wait for life to become perfect—recognize the good part while you’re living it.
