Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 54, “Underneath It All” contrasts two men who both claim their cause is justified, while their motives reveal the difference. Ammoron seeks power and control; Moroni fights to preserve freedom, faith, and family. Driven by gritty punk-blues, the song looks past speeches, labels, and righteous-sounding claims to ask a harder question: what do we actually want? Real discernment begins when we examine the motive underneath it all.
