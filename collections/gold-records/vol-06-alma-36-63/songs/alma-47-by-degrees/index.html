Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 47:18, “By Degrees” takes its title from the account of Lehonti, who was poisoned “by degrees.” The story becomes a powerful metaphor for how temptation and compromise often work—not all at once, but one small concession at a time. Set to a dark electro-swing and modern cabaret groove, the song gives voice to patient deception, exposing how truth is rarely abandoned in a single moment, but quietly traded away one step at a time.
