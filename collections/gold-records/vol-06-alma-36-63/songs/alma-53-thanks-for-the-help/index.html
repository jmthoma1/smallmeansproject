Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 53, “Thanks for the Help” finds humor and wisdom in an unexpected reversal: Moroni puts captured Lamanites to work strengthening the very fortifications designed to keep their armies out. With swaggering funk-rock energy, the song turns that irony into a modern lesson about adversity. We don’t always choose the pressure, opposition, or setbacks that come our way—but we can choose what they produce. Sometimes what was meant to weaken us becomes material for building something stronger.
