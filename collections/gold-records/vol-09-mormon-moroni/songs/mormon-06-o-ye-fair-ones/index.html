Behind the song

The Inspiration

A tender, minimalist piano duet inspired by Mormon's heartbreaking lament for his fallen people. An older male baritone and gentle female alto give voice to grief, love and the sorrow of lives lost. Delicate piano, subtle cello and intimate harmonies create a deeply personal farewell. Beneath the mourning lies Mormon's greatest heartbreak: his people turned away from the Savior, whose arms had always been open to receive them.
