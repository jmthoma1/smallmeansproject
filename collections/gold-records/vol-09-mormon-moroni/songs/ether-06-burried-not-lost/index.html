Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the Jaredite barges being swallowed beneath the waves yet continually brought back to the surface. The song reflects a powerful truth in Ether 6: being overwhelmed does not mean being abandoned. Even when the way forward is hidden and the pressure is intense, God still knows exactly where we are. What feels like burial may simply be part of the journey He is using to carry us home.
