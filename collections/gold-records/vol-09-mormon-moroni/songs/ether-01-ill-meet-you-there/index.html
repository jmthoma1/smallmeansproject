Behind the song

The Inspiration

I’ll Meet You There (Ether 1) A joyful Polynesian upa-upa anthem inspired by God's promise to meet the brother of Jared and lead his people to a new land. Rhythmic island drums, bamboo flute, conch calls and spirited male vocals create an adventurous journey of faith. Communal chants and infectious rhythms celebrate families gathering together, leaving confusion behind and trusting God's promise to guide them into the unknown.
