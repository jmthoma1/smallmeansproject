Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the final destruction of the Jaredite civilization, when generations of pride, vengeance, and war leave almost no one standing. The song strips away the idea of victory and asks the only question left at the end: what was any of it for? With kingdoms gone and enemies dead, Ether 15 becomes a warning about conflict pursued so long that winning loses all meaning.
