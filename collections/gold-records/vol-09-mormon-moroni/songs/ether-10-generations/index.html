Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the rise and fall of generations throughout Ether 10. Some learned to trust the Lord, some repeated the mistakes of those before them, and every generation had to decide for itself what truly mattered. The song turns that pattern toward us: history can teach us, but it cannot choose for us. Even when the people around us go the wrong direction, each of us still has to decide what we will believe, whom we will follow, and what kind of generation we will help create.
