Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the terrifying boast in Ether 14 that an army was sweeping the earth before it. The song turns that image into a warning about what happens when revenge becomes its own purpose. Cities fall, enemies scatter, and victory keeps advancing—but every triumph leaves less worth having. “Burn It All” asks the final question: if destruction is the only thing left standing, what exactly did you win?
