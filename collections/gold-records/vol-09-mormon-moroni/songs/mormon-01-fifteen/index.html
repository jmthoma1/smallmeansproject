Behind the song

The Inspiration

“Fifteen” (Mormon 1) A coming-of-age anthem about finding your identity and faith when the world around you is losing its way. At fifteen, Mormon discovers the goodness of Christ while surrounded by conflict and unbelief. Inspired by 2010s boy-band energy, the song captures youthful confidence, tight harmonies and the excitement of discovering that even when you're young, God can have a much bigger purpose for your life.
