Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspiration — “I Won’t Lead You There” (Mormon 3) Inspired by Mormon’s refusal to lead his people into a war of vengeance, despite his enduring love for them. A hard-driving outlaw country-rock anthem about drawing the line between loyalty and complicity. With gritty guitars, defiant vocals and a tender bridge, the song explores the courage to stand by your convictions, even when it means walking away from those you love.
