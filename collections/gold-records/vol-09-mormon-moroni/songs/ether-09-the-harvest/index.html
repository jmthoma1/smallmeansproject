Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the recurring Book of Mormon promise that righteousness brings prosperity in the land, while pride and rebellion eventually bring loss. Ether 9 shows both sides of that pattern through righteous kings, peace, abundance, corruption, famine, and destruction. “The Harvest” treats the chapter as a spiritual law of sowing and reaping: every people, every kingdom, and every heart eventually gathers what it has planted.
