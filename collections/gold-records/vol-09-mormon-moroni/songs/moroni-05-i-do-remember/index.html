Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the sacrament prayer on the wine and its declaration that disciples do always remember Him. The song treats the covenant as more than intention—it is a witness that remembrance has become part of daily life. “I Do Remember” is about carrying Christ beyond the sacrament table, letting His sacrifice shape ordinary choices, and returning again as evidence that the promise is still being lived.
