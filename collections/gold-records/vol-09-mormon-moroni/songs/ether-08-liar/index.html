Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s warning that secret combinations are built on the work of the father of lies—the same deceiver who has twisted truth, hardened hearts, and led people toward violence from the beginning. The song contrasts that darkness with the Lord’s way: open, honest, life-giving, and never dependent on secrecy, murder, or manipulation. “Liar” is ultimately about recognizing the oldest deception for what it is and choosing the light instead.
