Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s simple instructions for priests and teachers: preach repentance, remission of sins through Jesus Christ, and enduring faith in Him. The song reflects the idea that the gospel does not need to be dressed up to be powerful. Its message is already clear—come to Christ, trust His grace, and stay faithful to the end.
