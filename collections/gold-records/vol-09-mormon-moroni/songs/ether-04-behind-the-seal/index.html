Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s teaching that some truths remain sealed until people are spiritually prepared to receive them. Atmospheric prog pop/rock, shimmering guitars, layered synths, melodic bass and a soaring male vocal create a sense of mystery slowly opening into light. The song reflects the idea that God has not stopped revealing truth—there is still more behind the seal.
