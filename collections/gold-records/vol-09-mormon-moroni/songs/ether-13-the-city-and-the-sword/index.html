Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Ether’s vision of the New Jerusalem set against the violent collapse of the Jaredite kingdom. The song contrasts two ways of building: one through covenant, peace, and the Lord’s promises, the other through pride, power, and the sword. Ether 13 becomes a question for every generation and every heart—when heaven shows us a better way, what are we choosing to build?
