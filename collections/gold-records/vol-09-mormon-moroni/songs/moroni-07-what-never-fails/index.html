Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mormon’s teaching that faith, hope, and miracles all point toward something greater: charity, the pure love of Christ. The song builds toward the truth that while circumstances change and even faith eventually becomes knowledge, Christlike love endures. “What Never Fails” is about seeking the gift to love as He loves—the one thing that remains when everything else passes away.
