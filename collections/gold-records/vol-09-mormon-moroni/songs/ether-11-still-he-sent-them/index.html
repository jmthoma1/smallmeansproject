Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the repeated warnings God sent through prophets as the Jaredite nation moved deeper into pride, violence, and unbelief. Again and again, the people rejected the message, yet mercy kept reaching for them. The song focuses on the patience of God: before judgment came, He continued to send voices calling His people back. “Still He Sent Them” is a reminder that repeated warning is itself an expression of divine love.
