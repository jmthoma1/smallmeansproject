Behind the song

The Inspiration

“No Sail, No Anchor” (Mormon 5) Inspired by Mormon's comparison of his people to a ship without a sail or anchor, tossed helplessly by the waves. This driving Celtic maritime rock anthem blends rolling sea-shanty rhythms, energetic fiddle, electric guitars and rugged male harmonies. As the Nephites drift toward destruction, Mormon sends his testimony into the future, hoping others will find in Jesus Christ the direction and stability his people have lost.
