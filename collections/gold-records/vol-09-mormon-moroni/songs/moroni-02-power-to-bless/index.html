Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Christ giving His disciples authority to confer the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands. The chapter shows that sacred power is never meant for status or self-importance—it is given to strengthen, serve, and bless others. “Power to Bless” focuses on the responsibility that comes with receiving something from God: what heaven places in our hands is meant to become an act of love.
