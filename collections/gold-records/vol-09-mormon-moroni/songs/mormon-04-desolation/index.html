Behind the song

The Inspiration

A dark, female-fronted industrial electronic rock anthem exploring the bitter irony of a people fighting for a city called Desolation while bringing destruction upon themselves. Pulsating synthesizers, gritty guitars and haunting melodic vocals underscore a relentless cycle of pride, vengeance and hollow victories. As a civilization collapses, Mormon preserves the sacred records—a final act of hope amid the ruins.
