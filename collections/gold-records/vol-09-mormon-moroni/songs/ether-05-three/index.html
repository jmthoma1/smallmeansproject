Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s prophecy that three witnesses would see the sacred record and testify of its truth. The song reflects a broader scriptural pattern in which God confirms His work through multiple witnesses. From the Resurrection on the third day to the united witness of the Godhead, “Three” becomes a symbol of confirmation, completeness, and truth established by more than one voice.
