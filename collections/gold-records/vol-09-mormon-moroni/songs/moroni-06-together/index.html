Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s description of the Church as a covenant community where disciples are numbered, nourished by the word of God, remembered, prayed for, and strengthened through meeting together. “Together” celebrates the idea that following Christ is deeply personal but never meant to be solitary. We gather in His name to worship, repent, learn, and help carry one another along the way.
