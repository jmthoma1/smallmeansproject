The Inspiration

Inspiration — “Misplaced Sorrow” (Mormon 2) Inspired by Mormon's heartbreak when he discovers his people's sorrow comes from suffering rather than genuine repentance. Their tears initially bring him hope, but that hope turns to anguish when they refuse to change. Dark female progressive rock captures the painful difference between regretting consequences and truly turning back to God. A haunting exploration of false repentance, shattered hope and the tragedy of misplaced sorrow.
