Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the family divisions and struggles for power that begin to fracture the Jaredite kingdom. The song looks beyond the specific battles to the tragedy of pride turning those who share the same blood, history, and heritage against one another. Two voices sing together throughout, reflecting how both sides remain bound by what they share even while conflict pulls them apart.
