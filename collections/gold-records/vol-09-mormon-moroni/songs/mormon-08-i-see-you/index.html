Behind the song

The Inspiration

A flamboyant 1980s Hi-NRG dance-pop anthem inspired by Moroni's astonishing declaration that he has seen our day. Theatrical male vocals, relentless electronic beats and explosive synthesizers deliver a provocative warning about pride, wealth and religious hypocrisy. With infectious dance-floor energy and biting irony, Moroni confronts future generations, challenging them to look beyond appearances and care for those in need.
