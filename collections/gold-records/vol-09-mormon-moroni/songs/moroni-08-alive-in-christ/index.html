Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mormon’s teaching that little children are whole and alive in Christ through His mercy. The song rejects fear and misplaced condemnation, emphasizing that Christ’s grace already covers the innocent. “Alive in Christ” celebrates the joy of a Savior whose love is complete, whose mercy comes first, and whose perfect love casts out fear.
