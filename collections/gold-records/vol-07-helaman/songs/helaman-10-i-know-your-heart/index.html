Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 10 shows the Lord entrusting Nephi with extraordinary responsibility because He knew Nephi would not use that power for himself. “I Know Your Heart” imagines that moment from the Lord’s point of view. The song is about quiet faithfulness—the choices made when no one is watching, the willingness to listen, and the humility to carry responsibility without turning it into status. God can place more in our hands when He knows our hearts will remain centered on Him.
