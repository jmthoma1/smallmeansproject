Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 2 shows how dangerous evil becomes when it is protected by secrecy. “What Grows in the Dark” turns that warning inward. Small compromises, hidden resentments, dishonest choices, and private sins rarely stay small when they are continually concealed. The song is not about the power of darkness, but its weakness: once exposed to truth, accountability, and light, it begins to lose its hold.
