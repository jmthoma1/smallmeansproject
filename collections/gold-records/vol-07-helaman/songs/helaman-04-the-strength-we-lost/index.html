Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 4 shows a people who became weak long before they realized how much strength they had lost. “The Strength We Lost” compares that spiritual decline to physical atrophy. Strength fades when it is neglected, whether in the body or in faith. Prayer, scripture, repentance, service, and daily obedience may seem small, but they are the exercises that keep us spiritually strong. The hopeful message is that weakness is not permanent. When we return to those simple habits, strength can be rebuilt one choice at a time.
