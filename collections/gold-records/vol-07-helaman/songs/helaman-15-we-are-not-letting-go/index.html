Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 15:10 praises those who became “firm and steadfast in the faith.” “We Are Not Letting Go” brings that idea into the language of grip, endurance, and staying power. Faith can be tested by pressure, uncertainty, and time, but steadfastness means deciding what is worth holding onto and refusing to release it casually. The song is also an invitation to anyone starting to loosen their grip: we are still here, still holding on, and there is room to stand with us.
