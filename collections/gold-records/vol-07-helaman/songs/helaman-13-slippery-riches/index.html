Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 13 warns about “slippery riches”—wealth and possessions that cannot provide the security people expect from them. “Slippery Riches” turns that warning into a playful reminder that money and things are not the problem until they begin to own our attention, time, and heart. The cheerful barbershop style contrasts with the deeper message: what we clutch most tightly can still disappear, while the things of greatest worth are often the ones no lockbox can hold.
