Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 14 is filled with signs meant to warn, guide, and point people toward Christ. “Signs” brings that idea into everyday life. We trust stop signs, exit signs, warning labels, and detours because we understand that paying attention can keep us safe and help us reach where we are going. Spiritual signs work much the same way. God can warn, direct, and reveal, but He will not force us to follow. The song is about noticing the guidance already around us and choosing to act on it.
