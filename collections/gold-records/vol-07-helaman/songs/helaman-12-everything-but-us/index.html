Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 12 marvels that the earth, mountains, seas, and all creation respond to God’s command, while people so often resist Him. “Everything But Us” turns that contrast into a playful but pointed reflection on human stubbornness. Creation does not argue, negotiate, or demand every answer first—it simply responds. The song asks why we, with all our agency and understanding, can be the hardest part of creation to move, and ends with the hope that we can learn to listen too.
