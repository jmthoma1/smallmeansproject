Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspiration — Mosiah 21: Three Times Down Three crushing defeats leave Limhi’s people broken, grieving, and desperate. After relying on their own strength, they finally humble themselves and turn to God. Deliverance doesn’t come overnight, but their burdens gradually ease. Sometimes we have to fall before we learn to kneel, and sometimes God begins answering long before the chains come off.
