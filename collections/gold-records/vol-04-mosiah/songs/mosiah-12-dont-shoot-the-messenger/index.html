Behind the song

The Inspiration

A lone messenger walks into a room where power, wealth, and comfort have drowned out the voice of God. The warning he carries isn’t his own—it has already been written. Inspired by Mosiah 12, this cinematic noir ballad reminds us that rejecting the messenger never changes the message. Truth cannot be bribed, silenced, or delayed. It keeps knocking, waiting for every heart to decide whether to open the door.
