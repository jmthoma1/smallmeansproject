Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mosiah 24, “Stronger” celebrates the miracle of finding strength when life doesn’t get easier. As Alma’s people endured heavy burdens, the Lord didn’t remove every trial—He gave them the power to carry them. Blending vibrant Latin pop with an uplifting message of resilience, this anthem reminds us that faith doesn’t always change our circumstances, but it can change us into someone stronger than we ever imagined.
