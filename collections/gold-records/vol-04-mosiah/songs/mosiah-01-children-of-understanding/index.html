Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mosiah 1, “Children of Understanding” is a heartfelt country duet between parents and the next generation. A father speaks to his sons, a mother to her daughters, sharing timeless counsel about faith, wisdom, and truth. In a world full of noise and competing voices, the song invites children to seek understanding, treasure the teachings of those who came before, and grow into men and women of faith who will one day pass those truths on in love.
