Behind the song

The Inspiration

Zeniff believed he had found a better path. The road looked promising, the destination seemed right, and confidence made every mile feel like progress. But some roads lead back to lessons we thought we had already learned. Inspired by Mosiah 9, this song explores the danger of relying solely on our own understanding and the wisdom of seeking God’s direction. Wrong turns, second chances, and divine guidance remind us that while some roads lead back, others lead home.
