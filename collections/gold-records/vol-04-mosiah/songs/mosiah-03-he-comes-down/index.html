Behind the song

The Inspiration

In Mosiah chapter 3, an angel declares that the Lord Omnipotent will leave His heavenly throne and come among His people. He will heal the sick, comfort the weary, bear our sorrows, and suffer more than mortal words can describe. He Comes Down is a tender testimony of a Savior who does not remain distant. He comes down into our pain, our weakness, and our need—lifting us through His mercy, sacrifice, and redeeming love.
