Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mosiah 29, “Walk with the Wise” is a gritty blues-rock reminder that the people we choose to follow quietly shape the people we become. With driving guitars, swagger, and straight-shooting lyrics, the song warns that every road begins with a voice we decide to trust. Choosing good friends, wise mentors, and righteous influences can change the direction of a life long before anyone else notices.
