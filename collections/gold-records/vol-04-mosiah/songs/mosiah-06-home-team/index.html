Behind the song

The Inspiration

From Mosiah 6:1–2, After making a covenant with God, King Benjamin’s people have their names recorded and become known as the children of Christ. They are no longer wandering alone—they belong. This song celebrates the joy of being part of God’s family, united by faith, purpose, and covenant. Like a home team cheering one another forward, disciples of Christ stand together, lift each other, and press on toward the same eternal destination with hearts changed and names remembered.
