Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 2:28, “Right on Time” captures the moment the Nephites found themselves outnumbered and overwhelmed, yet turned to God in mighty prayer. Their circumstances didn’t disappear, but strength came when they needed it most. With a driving funk-rock groove and call-and-response energy, the song celebrates a timeless truth: when our own strength runs out, heaven can meet us where we stand—right on time.
