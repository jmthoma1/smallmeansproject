Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 16, this song is the lament of a prophet whose warning came true. Alma never wanted to be proven right—he wanted hearts to change. Looking over the ruins of Ammonihah, he grieves the tragedy of choices that never had to end this way. The song reminds us that God’s warnings are acts of love, given to spare us from sorrow, and that the greatest heartbreak is seeing mercy refused when there was still time.
