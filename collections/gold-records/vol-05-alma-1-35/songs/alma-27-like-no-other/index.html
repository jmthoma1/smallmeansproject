Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 27:27–31, “Like No Other” celebrates the remarkable character of the Anti-Nephi-Lehies. Their deep conversion produced a people filled with zeal toward God and love toward others—honest, upright, firm in faith, and fearless even in the face of death. Set to energetic British new wave and ska-pop, the song celebrates a different kind of strength: no weapons in their hands, no hatred in their hearts, and no turning back.
