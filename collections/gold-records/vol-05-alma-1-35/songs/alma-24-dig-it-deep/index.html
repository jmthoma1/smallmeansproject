Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 24, “Dig It Deep” captures the Anti-Nephi-Lehies burying their weapons as a covenant never to return to the violence of their past. With pounding industrial folk-rock and a gritty work-song rhythm, it turns their choice into a modern challenge: when mercy gives you a new beginning, don’t keep the old life within easy reach. Some things shouldn’t just be laid down. Dig them deep, leave them there, and walk away.
