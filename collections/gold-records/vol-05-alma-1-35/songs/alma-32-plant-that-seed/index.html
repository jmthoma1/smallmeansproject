Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 32, “Plant That Seed” turns Alma’s experiment of faith into a rowdy Southern-rock invitation: you don’t need perfect knowledge before you begin. Start with a desire to believe, give the word a place, and see what happens. If it swells, enlightens, and enlarges the soul, keep nourishing it. With gritty guitars and bar-band energy, the song celebrates faith as something you can test, grow, and eventually taste for yourself.
