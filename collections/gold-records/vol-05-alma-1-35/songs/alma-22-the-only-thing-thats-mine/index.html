Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 22:15–18, “The Only Thing That’s Mine” follows a king willing to surrender his possessions and even his kingdom to know God. But in offering everything, he discovers that none of those things were ever truly his. The real gift is something God will not take—his freedom to choose. With newfound understanding, he offers what only he can give: his will, his sins, and ultimately himself.
