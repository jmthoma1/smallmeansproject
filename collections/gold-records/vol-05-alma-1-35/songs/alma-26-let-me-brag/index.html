Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 26, “Let Me Brag” captures Ammon’s overflowing joy as he looks back on the miracles of their mission. When accused of boasting, he gladly admits it—but gives all the credit to God. Set to an infectious disco-funk celebration, the song rejoices in changed hearts, unexpected success, and strength given in moments of weakness. Ammon knows what he accomplished alone was small, but he can’t stay quiet about what God has done.
