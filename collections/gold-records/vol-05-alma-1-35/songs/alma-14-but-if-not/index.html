Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 14, “But If Not” is an upbeat declaration of unwavering faith in the face of life’s hardest moments. Following the timeless example of faithful believers who trusted God regardless of the outcome, the song reminds us that faith doesn’t promise a life without storms—it gives us the courage to keep singing through them. The story is always bigger than this chapter, and God’s promises remain even when our circumstances don’t change.
