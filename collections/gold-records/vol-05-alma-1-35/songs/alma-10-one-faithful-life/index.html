Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 10, One Faithful Life celebrates the quiet influence of ordinary disciples. Through Amulek’s testimony, we learn that the prayers and faithfulness of the righteous bless far more people than they may ever realize. With bright, uplifting pop energy, the song reminds us that God multiplies every act of faith, every whispered prayer, and every willing heart. One faithful life, surrendered to Him, can change countless others.
