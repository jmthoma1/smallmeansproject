Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 29, “If I Were an Angel” captures Alma’s longing to proclaim God’s goodness with a voice powerful enough for the whole world to hear. But his desire gives way to humility as he recognizes that God has already given him a voice, a place, and a work of his own. A rolling Polynesian-inspired 6/8 groove gives the song a spacious, ceremonial warmth, carrying Alma from his grandest wish to a quieter truth: he doesn’t need to be an angel for his voice to matter.
