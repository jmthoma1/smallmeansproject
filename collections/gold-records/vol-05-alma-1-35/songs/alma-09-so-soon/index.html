Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 9, So Soon reflects on how easily hearts can forget the Lord’s goodness after experiencing His blessings. With bright, nostalgic British pop energy contrasting its searching lyrics, the song asks a timeless question: How do we forget the God who has carried us so many times? More than a warning, it is an invitation to remember His faithfulness, cherish His mercies, and keep gratitude alive before memory quietly fades.
