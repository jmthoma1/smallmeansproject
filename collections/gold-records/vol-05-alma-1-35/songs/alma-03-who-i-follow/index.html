Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 3:27, “Who I Follow” explores the quiet truth that every choice shapes who we become. Every voice we trust, every path we take, and every step we repeat leaves its mark on our hearts. Set to a warm, harmony-rich California folk-rock sound, the song invites listeners to pause and consider the direction of their lives, reminding us that our destination is determined one decision at a time by who—and what—we choose to follow.
