Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 15, “Yesterday Isn’t Driving Anymore” celebrates the freedom that comes when grace takes the wheel. Zeezrom finds healing, Amulek finds a new home, and both discover that their past no longer defines their future. With an uplifting road-trip spirit, the song reminds us that faith doesn’t erase yesterday—it simply refuses to let it steer tomorrow. Every sunrise is another invitation to move forward with hope, courage, and Christ
