Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 21, “Still Knockin’” celebrates Aaron and his brethren carrying the gospel forward despite rejection, opposition, and imprisonment. With a playful bubblegum-pop and female rap crossover, the song brings their determination into the present day. Not everyone will listen or believe, but everyone deserves the chance to hear. The messenger’s job is to share, serve, and love—then keep moving forward and keep knockin’.
