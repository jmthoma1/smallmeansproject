Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 34:32–35, “Not Tomorrow” turns Amulek’s warning against procrastinating repentance into an energetic call to live and change today. We can spend our lives waiting for the perfect time, but tomorrow is always one day away. Driven by urgent piano rock, the song celebrates the time we actually have: this day, this moment, this chance to turn our hearts toward God. Not someday. Not tomorrow. Today.
