Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 1, “Not for Sale” celebrates the truth that God’s greatest gifts can never be bought, marketed, or earned. In a world where influence, success, and even faith are often treated like commodities, the gospel still comes “without money and without price.” Featuring an uplifting all-female bluegrass sound, the song contrasts the empty promises of pride with the quiet power of grace, generosity, and truth that will always remain not for sale.
