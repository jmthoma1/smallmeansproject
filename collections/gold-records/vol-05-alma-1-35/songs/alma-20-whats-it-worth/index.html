Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 20, this Western Swing story follows a meeting between a king and a servant, where wealth, power, freedom, and loyalty quietly reveal what each man treasures most. Without preaching, the song lets every offer and every response speak for itself. As the dust settles, listeners are left to ask what truly lasts—and what they themselves have been measuring all along
