Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 12:9–11, “Use It or Lose It” explores a simple truth: what we do with the understanding we’ve already received shapes what comes next. When we follow truth, our capacity to recognize more can grow; when we continually neglect it, even what once seemed clear can fade. Set to bright, quirky 1980s British new wave, the song turns a profound spiritual principle into a playful reminder—use what you’ve been given.
