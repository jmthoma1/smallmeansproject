Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 23, “Never, No Never” celebrates the Anti-Nephi-Lehies and the depth of a faith that transformed how they lived. They didn’t simply hear the truth—they took it in, planted it deep, and let it change their direction. Set to a high-energy Appalachian string-band sound with fiddle, banjo, jaw harp, and raw group harmonies, the song asks what makes faith last: knowing the Lord, living what you believe, and giving Him your whole heart.
