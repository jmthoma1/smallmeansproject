Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 14, “Not That Complicated” leans into the plainness of Christ’s teaching: bad fruit is bad fruit, sand is a bad foundation, and obvious flaws should be faced honestly. The song applies that directness to modern life, where we often rename, excuse, or overcomplicate what we already know. Its roots-rock sound gives the message energy, grit, and a little wry common sense.
