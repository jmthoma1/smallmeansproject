Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 26, “What Will You Do With It?” explores Christ’s declaration that He will try the faith of His people. God has greater truths to reveal, but first asks us to act on what we’ve already received. Whether it’s a prompting, a gift, or a glimpse of truth, faith grows through action. Its psychedelic funk and progressive pop sound brings playful energy and curiosity to a challenging question: what are you doing with the light you already have?
