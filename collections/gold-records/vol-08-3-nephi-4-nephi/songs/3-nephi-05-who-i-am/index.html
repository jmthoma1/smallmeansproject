Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 5, “Who I Am” centers on Mormon’s simple declaration that he is a disciple of Jesus Christ. The song reflects on identity, purpose, and the hope that what we leave behind points others toward the Savior. Its cowboy sound gives the message a plainspoken, grounded sincerity—less about status or recognition, and more about knowing who you are and what you stand for.
