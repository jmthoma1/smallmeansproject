Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 30, “Turn Around” transforms Christ’s invitation to repent, be baptized, and receive the Holy Ghost into an exuberant celebration of second chances. No matter how far down the wrong road we've traveled, we can change direction and come unto Him. With lightning-fast ska rhythms, bouncing bass, offbeat guitars, explosive brass, and spirited male vocals, this energetic album finale reminds us that the road back begins with a decision to turn around.
