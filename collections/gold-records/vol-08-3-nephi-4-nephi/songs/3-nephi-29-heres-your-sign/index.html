Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 29, “Here's Your Sign” delivers Mormon's urgent warning: the Book of Mormon is a sign that God is fulfilling His ancient promises. Some recognize it, while others refuse to see what's right in front of them. With blistering punkabilly guitar, frantic piano, slap bass, and rapid-fire female vocals, this high-speed song challenges those who dismiss continuing revelation, deny miracles, or ignore God's covenant promises.
