Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 4 Nephi, “The Day We Drew the Lines” traces the tragic unraveling of a society once united in Christ, where there were no poor, no divisions, and all things were shared. As prosperity gives way to pride, small distinctions become walls that tear a people apart. Beginning with warm, harmonious folk-rock and building into explosive alternative rock, the music mirrors a powerful warning: unity is rarely lost all at once, but one line at a time.
