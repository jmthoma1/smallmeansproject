Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 15, “Law and Light” reflects Christ’s teaching that the law was never the final destination—it was always pointing toward Him. The song contrasts signs, symbols, rules, and forms with the deeper purpose beneath them. Its female-led progressive folk-rock style gives the message an adventurous, intelligent energy while keeping the focus on Christ as both fulfillment and light.
