Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 10, “Under His Wings” reflects Christ’s repeated invitation to gather, protect, and receive those willing to come to Him. The song focuses on mercy that keeps reaching even after rejection, wandering, and loss. Its all-female a cappella sound gives the message a tender, communal warmth, as if many voices are answering the same call to refuge.
