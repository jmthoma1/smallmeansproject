Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 12, “A Better Way” translates Christ’s teachings into modern situations—retaliation, resentment, pride, desire, and selective love. The song contrasts what the world tells us to do with the higher way Christ teaches. Its bluegrass style gives the message an earthy, direct energy, making hard teachings feel practical, memorable, and lived-in.
