Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 27, “As I Am” reflects Christ’s invitation to become like Him. Bearing His name means more than professing belief—it means living His gospel through forgiveness, kindness, and everyday choices. Its lively boogie-woogie rhythm, swinging piano, and punchy brass bring joyful energy to a lifelong challenge: we come to Christ as we are, but He invites us to become as He is.
