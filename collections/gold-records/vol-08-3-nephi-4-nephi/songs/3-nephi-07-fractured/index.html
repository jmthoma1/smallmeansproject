Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 7, “Fractured” reflects a society broken into tribes, loyalties, and competing identities while Nephi continues gathering people to Christ. The song applies that tension to today: divisions may be real, but discipleship offers an identity deeper than faction or label. Its moody post-punk sound mirrors a world splintering while one steady voice keeps calling.
