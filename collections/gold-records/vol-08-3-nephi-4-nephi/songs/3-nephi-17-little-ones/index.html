Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 17, “Little Ones” centers on the moment Christ gathers and blesses the children as heaven seems to open around them. The song reflects innocence, tenderness, and the closeness of the Savior to those the world might overlook. Its layered vocal-group style gives the scene a luminous, almost heavenly quality, with harmony carrying the sense of wonder.
