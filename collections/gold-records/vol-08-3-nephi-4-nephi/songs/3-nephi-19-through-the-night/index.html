Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 19, “Through the Night” focuses on the people who traveled through the darkness to spread the news and gather others before morning. The song reflects the urgency of good news that feels too important to keep private. Its driving Celtic folk-rock style gives the story momentum, warmth, and the feeling of a whole community moving together toward something sacred.
