Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 28, “Still Here” imagines the Three Nephites carrying Christ’s message across centuries as kingdoms rise, cities fall, and generations pass. Their extraordinary desire to remain on earth and bring souls to Him becomes a testament to unwavering purpose. Atmospheric 80s synth-rock, pulsing rhythms, and soaring guitars capture a world constantly changing while their mission remains the same: serving until Christ comes again.
