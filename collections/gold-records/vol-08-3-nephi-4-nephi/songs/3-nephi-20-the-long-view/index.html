Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 20, “The Long View” reflects how God’s covenant work stretches across generations, often beyond what one lifetime can see. The song contrasts our short perspective with His wider one: we see a wave, He sees the tide; we see a moment, He sees the horizon. Its warm Polynesian-influenced folk style gives the message patience, openness, and quiet trust.
