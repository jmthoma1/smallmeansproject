Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 3, “Who Do You Think We Are?” captures the sheer audacity of Giddianhi’s demand that the Nephites surrender, join him, and accept his terms as though resistance were unreasonable. The song focuses less on the threat itself and more on the arrogance behind it—the assumption that if enough of the world accepts darkness, everyone else should simply fall in line. Its minor-key rockabilly style gives that defiance a sharp, almost taunting edge, while the female lead turns the response into something cool, incredulous, and unafraid.
