Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 13, “The Quiet Things” focuses on the unseen parts of discipleship—private prayer, unrecognized kindness, hidden sacrifice, and choices made without applause. The song reflects Christ’s teaching that what matters most is often done in secret, seen only by God. Its raspy female soul style gives that message warmth, honesty, and lived-in conviction.
