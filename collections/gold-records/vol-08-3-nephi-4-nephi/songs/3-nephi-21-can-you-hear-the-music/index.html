Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 21, “Can You Hear the Music?” uses music as a metaphor for recognizing that God’s promised work has already begun. The chapter points to the coming forth of these words as a sign of the gathering. The song asks whether we are still waiting for a dramatic beginning while the first notes are already sounding. Its bright indie-rock style gives that realization energy, motion, and discovery.
