Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 2, “The Enemy of My Enemy” focuses on the moment when old divisions become secondary to a common threat. The song explores how survival can force people to see beyond history, pride, and former loyalties, revealing that shared purpose can unite those who once stood apart. Its tribal percussion and modern alt-rock sound give the song a primal urgency, as separate sides move toward one cause.
