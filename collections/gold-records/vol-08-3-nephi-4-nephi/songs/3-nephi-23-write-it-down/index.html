Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 23, “Write It Down” reflects Christ’s command to search the scriptures and record fulfilled prophecy. The song connects that lesson to preserving personal experiences of God’s hand so future generations can learn from them. Its hip-hop and cowboy crossover duet blends rhythmic storytelling with down-to-earth wisdom, turning two musical voices into one message: read what was written, recognize what came true, and write it down.
