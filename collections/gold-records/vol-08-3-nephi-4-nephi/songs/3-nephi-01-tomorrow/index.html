Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 1:13, “Tomorrow” explores how truth does not need our approval to become real. Using time, signals, patterns, and inevitability, it reflects how prophecy may seem distant until fulfillment brings it into focus. Its progressive-rock sound captures that restless tension, while the chorus returns to one simple truth: tomorrow keeps arriving, carrying every word with it.
