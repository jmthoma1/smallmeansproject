Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 22, “Stretch the Tent” turns Isaiah’s imagery of enlarging the tent into a song about preparing for restoration before it arrives. The message is simple: don’t build only for what you’ve lost or what you can see now. Make room for what God still intends to do. Its female-led orchestral indie-folk sound grows as the song does, mirroring faith that expands before the promise is fulfilled.
