Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 18, “Make Room” brings together Christ’s teachings on remembrance, prayer, gathering, and welcoming those who are still searching. The song focuses on making space—for the Savior, for worship, and for people who may not yet feel ready or certain. Its jangly indie-rock style gives the message a thoughtful, human warmth without sounding devotional or polished.
