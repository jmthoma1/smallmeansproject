Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 16, “The Shepherd” reflects Christ’s assurance that His care reaches far beyond the people and places we can see. The song centers on a simple truth: no flock owns the Shepherd, and no soul is outside His knowledge. Its Southern Gospel Swing style gives the message warmth, dignity, and plainspoken confidence.
