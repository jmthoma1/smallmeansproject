Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 11, “One by One” captures the extraordinary moment when Christ invites each person to come forward and know Him personally. Though He appeared to a multitude, no one was lost in the crowd. The song reflects how testimony ultimately becomes individual—we each must come, hear, and know for ourselves. Its piano-rock build from solo voice to full choir mirrors many individual witnesses becoming one joyful testimony.
