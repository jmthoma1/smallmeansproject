Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 25, “Fathers and Sons” celebrates Elijah’s promise to turn hearts across generations, uniting families through sacred covenants and temple sealing. Its vibrant African pop choir transforms the message into a joyful celebration of belonging, with infectious rhythms, rich harmonies, and communal voices expressing the hope that those who came before and those yet to come can be bound together forever.
