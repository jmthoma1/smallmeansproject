Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder Michael John U. Teh’s April 2026 Saturday Morning Session General Conference message titled “Follow the Prophet; He Knows the Way.” Sunlight reflected between Hong Kong buildings becomes an image of how prophets point us to Christ and how we can reflect His light.
