The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder Pedro X. Larreal’s April 2026 Sunday Morning Session General Conference message titled “I Feel My Savior’s Love.” The song quiets a stream of digital voices to make room for the Savior’s love and the remembrance the sacrament offers.
