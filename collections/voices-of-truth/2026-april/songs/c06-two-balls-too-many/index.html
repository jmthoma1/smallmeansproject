The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder Dale G. Renlund’s April 2026 Sunday Morning Session General Conference message titled “Because of Jesus Christ.” A poodle juggling yellow balls asks what happens when lesser pursuits crowd out our attention to Jesus Christ.
