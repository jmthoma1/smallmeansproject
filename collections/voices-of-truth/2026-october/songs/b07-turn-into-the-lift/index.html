Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder Patrick Kearon’s October 2026 Saturday Afternoon Session General Conference message titled "Jesus Lives to Lift." The song uses the image of lift to explore what it means to turn toward Christ when we feel weighed down.
