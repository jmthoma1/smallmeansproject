Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder James G. O. Fantone’s October 2026 Sunday Morning Session General Conference message titled "Take Upon Us His Name." An ordinary name grounds the song’s question about how taking Christ’s name changes the way we serve a particular person.
