Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder Kevin J. Hathaway’s October 2026 Saturday Morning Session General Conference message titled "Jesus Christ Is the Great Deliverer!." The song uses two tires to tell a story of deliverance and to ask where we turn when we cannot get through a trial alone.
