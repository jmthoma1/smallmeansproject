Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Sister Andrea Muñoz Spannaus’s October 2026 Sunday Afternoon Session General Conference message titled "God Is at Your Side." The song turns a direction into a question about recognizing and following God’s guidance in an ordinary decision.
