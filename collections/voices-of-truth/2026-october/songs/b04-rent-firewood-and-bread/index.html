The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder Patricio M. Giuffra’s October 2026 Saturday Afternoon Session General Conference message titled "Trusting the Lord Through Tithes and Offerings." Rent, firewood, and bread put the choice to pay tithing in the middle of real household needs.
