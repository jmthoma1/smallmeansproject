Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder Jeremiah J. Morgan’s October 2026 Saturday Afternoon Session General Conference message titled "The Infinite Power of God in Our Covenants." The song looks across generations to consider how one person’s covenants can bless a family long after that person is gone.
