Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Elder David A. Bednar’s October 2026 Sunday Afternoon Session General Conference message titled "Sealed by the Holy Spirit of Promise." The image of a stamp points toward the promise of covenants and the place of the Holy Spirit in making that promise sure.
