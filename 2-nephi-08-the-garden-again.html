Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the promise in Second Nephi Chapter 8 (Isaiah 51) that the Lord will comfort Zion and make “her wilderness like Eden.” “The Garden Again” is a joyful celebration of restoration, renewal, and hope after sorrow. Using warm vintage harmonies and blooming garden imagery, the song reflects Isaiah’s vision of singing, rejoicing, and life returning where there was once only emptiness and desolation.
