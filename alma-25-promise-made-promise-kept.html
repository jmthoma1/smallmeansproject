Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 25, “Promise Made, Promise Kept” celebrates faithfulness on every side. The Lord fulfills His word in every detail. The missionaries keep their commitment to serve and finally see the fruits of their labor. The converts remain true to the promises that changed their lives. Set to joyful, sophisticated big-band swing, the song reminds us that promises connect heaven and earth: say what you mean, live what you believe, and follow through.
