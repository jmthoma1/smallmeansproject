Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 13, “His Way” explores the pattern of discipleship that precedes every divine calling. Before anyone is entrusted to lead, they learn to follow. Before hands are lifted in service, hearts are shaped through quiet faith, unseen choices, and steadfast obedience. The song celebrates a life patterned after the Son of God—not seeking honor, but becoming someone God can trust to lift, serve, teach, and lead others in His way.
