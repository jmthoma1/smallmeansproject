Four Years at the Beach

Ether 2 · Small Means Project

Behind the song

The Inspiration

A breezy, humorous tropical country-rock song inspired by the Jaredites spending four years at the seashore, forgetting to continue their journey. With laid-back vocals, steel drums, acoustic guitars and sunny island rhythms, the song captures the temptation to get comfortable halfway to God's promises. A three-hour wake-up call from heaven reminds them that paradise isn't always the destination—and it's time to build some boats!
