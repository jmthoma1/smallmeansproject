Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 4, “So Long As We Call” reflects the people’s recognition that preparation alone was not enough. Their safety depended on continuing to call on God and remain righteous. The song applies that lesson today: planning and effort matter, but they should never replace humility, prayer, and trust in the true source of strength.
