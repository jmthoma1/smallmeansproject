Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 17 (Isaiah 7), Looking Everywhere is a playful yet thoughtful reflection on the human tendency to search for answers in plans, experts, money, power, and worry while overlooking the One source of lasting peace. As fear and uncertainty drive people in circles, the song reminds us that God has not abandoned us. The answer we seek is often closer than we realize—present, patient, and waiting right beside us, right here and right now.
