Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mosiah 13:34–35, “He Himself” captures Abinadi’s bold declaration to Noah’s priests: they knew the Scriptures, but they missed the Savior those Scriptures proclaimed. The law was never the destination—it was always pointing to Jesus Christ. Not another prophet. Not another sacrifice. God Himself would come down among the children of men and pay the price of redemption. The greatest revelation was standing before them all along.
