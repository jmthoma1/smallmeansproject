Behind the song

The Inspiration

“Nevertheless” is inspired by 1 Nephi 11:17, where Nephi is shown visions of the Savior and asked questions beyond his understanding. Instead of pretending certainty, he answers with humility: “I know that he loveth his children; nevertheless, I do not know the meaning of all things.” This piano-driven narrative is about trusting God without having every answer and finding peace in the face of Christ.
