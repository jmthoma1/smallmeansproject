Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 1 Nephi 18:11–21, “Loose the Cords” is a haunting spiritual rock ballad told from the perspective of Nephi’s wife during the great storm at sea. As fear, pride, and chaos threaten to destroy the family, she pleads for Nephi to be freed so God can guide them once more. A powerful song about faith, revelation, and trusting God through life’s darkest storms.
