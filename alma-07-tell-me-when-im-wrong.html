Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 7:22–24, “Tell Me When I’m Wrong” explores what it means to be humble, gentle, patient, and “easy to be entreated.” With playful Hot Club swing and a touch of self-deprecating humor, the song asks whether we’re truly willing to listen, learn, and admit when we’ve missed something. Following Christ means more than having answers—it means keeping a heart open enough to be corrected, changed, and taught.
