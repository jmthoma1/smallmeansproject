Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 6, “Different Tables” reflects how quickly unity can fracture when prosperity turns into pride. The song uses the image of people separating themselves by status, wealth, and importance to show how abundance can become spiritually dangerous when it teaches us to measure one another instead of love one another.
