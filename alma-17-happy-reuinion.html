Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 17:1–4, “Happy Reunion” captures the joy of Alma unexpectedly reuniting with the sons of Mosiah after years apart. Before the missionary journeys continue, there is a moment of gratitude, friendship, and celebration. Set to the infectious energy of a New Orleans Second Line brass band, the song reminds us that God not only guides our paths but also blesses us with joyful reunions that strengthen our faith and renew our hope.
