Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 6, Until They Know celebrates a church united in purpose. After embracing the gospel, the disciples organize, gather often, and continue their work through fasting, prayer, and love for those who have not yet come to know God. Rather than turning inward, they lift one another and faithfully seek the welfare of every soul, reminding us that true discipleship means walking together until everyone has the opportunity to know Christ.
