Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 59, “Before It Breaks” draws from Moroni’s realization that it would have been far easier to protect Nephihah than to regain it after it was lost. With a lively rockabilly/cowboy sound, the song turns that lesson into practical advice for modern life: maintain what matters while you still have it. Fix the small problem, make the call, mend the relationship, heed the warning. A little care today can save a much harder recovery tomorrow.
