Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by the sacrament prayer on the bread and especially the promise to be willing to take Christ’s name upon us, always remember Him, and keep His commandments. The song focuses on willingness rather than perfection: bringing Christ a sincere heart, choosing Him again and again, and carrying that promise beyond the sacrament table into everyday life.
