Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 31 and the Zoramites’ prideful worship, “I’m Better Than You” uses bright female barbershop harmony to expose the absurdity of thanking God for being superior to everyone else. They climb the Rameumptom, repeat the same prayer, celebrate being chosen, then forget God for the rest of the week. Beneath the cheerful harmonies is a warning about pride: when we’re certain God agrees with us, we may stop asking whether we need to change.
