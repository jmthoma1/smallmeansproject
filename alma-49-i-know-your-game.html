Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 49, “I Know Your Game” explores the power of knowing an enemy before the attack ever comes. Moroni anticipates where the Lamanites will strike and prepares accordingly, leaving them powerless to exploit an apparent weakness. Driven by industrial pop-rock, the song brings that principle into modern life: know what threatens you, recognize its tactics, strengthen the vulnerable places, and prepare before temptation, pressure, or trouble arrives.
