Behind the song

The Inspiration

A high-energy male/female rap-funk crossover inspired by Moroni's challenge to those who believe miracles have ceased. Witty old-school rap, powerhouse female gospel vocals, slap bass, funky guitars and punchy brass deliver a joyful reminder that God is the same yesterday, today and forever. With playful banter and infectious rhythms, the song declares that heaven never closed its doors—God is still in the miracle business!
