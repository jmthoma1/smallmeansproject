Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 24, “Turn Up the Heat” explores Christ as the Refiner, patiently removing impurities to reveal the gold within us. The song reflects our willingness to let God transform our weaknesses into strength, even when growth is uncomfortable. Its gritty blues-funk groove, industrial percussion, and powerful female vocals capture the intensity of refinement and the joy of becoming something brighter.
