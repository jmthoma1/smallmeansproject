Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 11 compresses the pride cycle into a single chapter: prosperity, pride, conflict, suffering, humility, repentance, blessing—and then the pattern begins again. “The Cycle” turns that pattern into a modern warning about how easily we remember God when life is hard and forget Him when life becomes comfortable. The song asks whether we can break the cycle by staying grateful, humble, and faithful not only in the drought, but also when the rain comes.
