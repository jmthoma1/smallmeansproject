Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Mormon’s refusal to stop serving even as his people descend into violence, cruelty, and spiritual numbness. He knows he may not be able to change them, but he also knows their choices do not excuse him from doing what is right. “Still We Labor” is about remaining faithful when the work feels hopeless, holding onto Christ when the world grows darker, and trusting His power to lift us when the burden becomes too heavy.
