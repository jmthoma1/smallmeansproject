Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 3:25 describes blessings so abundant that even the high priests and teachers were astonished beyond measure. “No Limits” captures that sense of joyful surprise. It is about looking for God’s goodness and discovering something even greater than expected—mercy, strength, peace, and blessings that cannot be neatly measured. The song celebrates the idea that even after years of faith, God can still leave us amazed.
