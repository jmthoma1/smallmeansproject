Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 51, “Same Side” explores what happens when disagreement turns into something more destructive. The king-men become so opposed to their own countrymen that their resistance ultimately benefits the enemy threatening them all. Driven by a fast male/female pop-punk duet, the song brings that warning into modern life: disagree, argue, and stand your ground—but don’t become so determined to see someone else lose that you forget their loss may be yours too.
