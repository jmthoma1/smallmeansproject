Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 8, Go Back captures the joy and urgency of immediate obedience. After being rejected, Alma is told to return—and he goes back speedily, trusting God before he understands the reason. Unknown to him, the Lord has already prepared the way. Set to an energetic rock-and-roll groove, the song celebrates the faith to turn around, follow God’s call without hesitation, and discover the miracles He has already set in motion.
