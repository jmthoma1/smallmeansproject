Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 16 recounts Samuel standing on the wall as stones and arrows repeatedly miss him. “Missed Again” retells the scene through Mormon’s hindsight, exploring how people can witness signs yet dismiss them when belief demands change. The title reflects two kinds of missing: Samuel’s attackers miss their target, while others miss the meaning of what they see. How many coincidences can we accept before we look again?
