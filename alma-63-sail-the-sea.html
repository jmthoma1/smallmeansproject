Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 63, “Sail the Sea” celebrates Hagoth and the families who built ships, left familiar shores, and ventured toward a future they could not see. Wrapped in bright, nostalgic doo-wop harmonies, the song captures the excitement of stepping into the unknown with preparation, faith, and a willingness to go. Some destinations can’t be discovered from the safety of the shore—sometimes you have to raise the sail, catch the breeze, and see where it takes you.
