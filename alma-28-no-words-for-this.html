Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 28:8, “No Words for This” celebrates a joy so profound that ordinary language can’t contain it—what Mormon calls “incomprehensible joy.” A bright reggae/rap crossover turns that idea into an infectious celebration: try calling it peace, hope, mercy, or love, and every word still comes up short. When God changes hearts, brings former enemies together, and gives hope beyond sorrow, sometimes words run out—and joy takes over.
