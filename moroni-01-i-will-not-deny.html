Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s quiet, unwavering refusal to deny Christ even when doing so could cost him his life. Alone, hunted, and believing his record was nearly finished, he still chooses faith over safety and keeps writing for people he may never meet. The song is about conviction that survives loss—when almost everything else is gone, testimony remains.
