Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Moroni’s examples of men who acted in faith before they ever saw the miracle. Alma and Amulek stood firm, Nephi and Lehi trusted God, Ammon and his brethren went where faith led them, the three disciples held to Christ’s promise, and the brother of Jared believed beyond the veil. “Rockstars of Faith” celebrates the courage to move first, trust first, and believe first—because the witness comes after the trial of faith.
