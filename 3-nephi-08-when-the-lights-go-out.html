Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 8, “When the Lights Go Out” explores what remains when the things we trust suddenly fail. The chapter’s earthquakes, destruction, and impenetrable darkness become a modern metaphor for moments when plans, systems, and self-reliance can no longer carry us. Its futuristic trap-rock sound gives the song a tense, physical edge while the chorus asks what still stands when everything else is gone.
