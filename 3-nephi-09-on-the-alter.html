Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 3 Nephi 9, “On the Altar” centers on Christ’s call for a broken heart and contrite spirit instead of outward sacrifice. The song turns that invitation inward, asking what pride, fear, anger, or hidden pain we are willing to place before Him. The lone male voice and electric cello give the message a personal, exposed intensity—simple, vulnerable, and direct.
