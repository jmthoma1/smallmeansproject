Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 19, “Overcome” captures the extraordinary moment when King Lamoni, the queen, Ammon, and others are overcome by the Spirit. To those watching, they appear defeated and fallen, but something powerful is happening within. Set to an energetic K-pop and Japanese fusion, the song turns the meaning of “overcome” upside down: what looked like an ending was a beginning. They fell—but heaven wasn’t finished. Then they rose
