Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 30 and Korihor’s confession, “I Can Lie to Myself” explores the danger of repeating a lie until you become its believer. Korihor knew the truth at first, but success, praise, and repetition slowly buried what he knew. Driven by raw psychedelic blues-rock, the song begins with swagger and ends in regret: he could deceive the crowd and eventually himself, but when everything fell apart, the truth remained—he had known all along.
