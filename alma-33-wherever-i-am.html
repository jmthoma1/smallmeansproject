Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 33 and Zenos’s prayer, “Wherever I Am” celebrates the truth that prayer isn’t confined to a church or sacred place. In the field, at home, alone, or among others, God hears those who reach for Him. A bright Balkan brass and jangle-pop duet gives the song a colorful, joyful energy, reflecting a simple assurance: no walls, distance, or circumstance can prevent us from calling on God. Wherever we are, He hears us.
