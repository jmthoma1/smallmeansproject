Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by 2 Nephi 33, Even Though We’ve Never Met imagines Nephi’s final message as a personal letter carried across centuries to future readers. With warmth, humility, and deep charity, he shares the truths and tender mercies that sustained him through life’s trials, hoping they will bless others he will never meet in mortality. The song celebrates faith, friendship, testimony, and the remarkable connection forged through scripture, ending with the hopeful promise that those who follow Christ
