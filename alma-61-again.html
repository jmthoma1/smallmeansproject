Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 61, “Again” reflects the return of an enemy that had already been defeated. Like recurring sin, addiction, or destructive habits, some battles demand continued vigilance—and sometimes they return despite our best efforts. Driven by dark alternative rock and trip-hop, the song turns “again” from a word of discouragement into one of hope: repent again, ask for help again, get up again. When the fight becomes too much to face alone, there is strength in calling for reinforcements.
