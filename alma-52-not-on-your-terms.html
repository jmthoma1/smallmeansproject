Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 52, “Not on Your Terms” explores the wisdom of refusing to fight where an opponent holds the advantage. Rather than attacking fortified cities head-on, Moroni and his commanders change the battlefield. Blending moody trip-hop with explosive alternative rock, the song brings that strategy into modern life: not every challenge, argument, or provocation deserves an immediate response. Strength isn’t avoiding the fight—it’s choosing when, where, and how to engage.
