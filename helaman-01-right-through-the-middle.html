Behind the song

The Inspiration

Helaman 1 warns how quickly distraction can become vulnerability. “Right Through the Middle” brings that lesson into modern life: when our attention is scattered, divided, or consumed by things that matter less, the enemy does not always need to overpower us—he simply looks for the opening we stopped guarding. The frantic, unexpected energy of the song mirrors that danger: life can feel chaotic, loud, and almost playful right up until something important slips through the middle.
