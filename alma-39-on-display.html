Behind the song

The Inspiration

Inspired by Alma 39:11, “On Display” explores Alma’s warning that Corianton’s choices affected more than himself—they influenced how others received the message he was supposed to represent. With theatrical glam-rock and funk energy, the song turns that lesson into a reminder about integrity: our lives can speak louder than our words. Whether anyone seems to be watching or not, what we choose reveals who we are—and may influence someone more than we realize.
